Simple by design

From a spoken thought
to another voice.

The experience is meant to feel simple. The research behind it takes care to honour speech, meaning and the people who speak it.

Language is more than words

Technology should listen with respect.

Yorùbá carries knowledge through everyday conversation, stories and generations of speaking. This project explores speech technology designed with that living oral tradition in mind.
